Run a review of the second brain:

1. **Inbox:** for each note in `second_brain/00_inbox/` (except README), propose where it belongs (project, area, resource, decision, lesson, archive). Show me the list, move them after I agree.
2. **Stale notes:** list notes with `status: draft` older than 14 days, and project notes whose content contradicts a newer decision.
3. **Broken links:** list `[[links]]` that point to notes that do not exist.
4. **Summary:** 3–5 bullets on what changed since the last review (use `git log` if available).
5. Ask before deleting anything; prefer moving to `40_archive/`.
