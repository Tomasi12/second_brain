# MATyHOUSE — product ideas and start plan

## Summary
Not decided yet what to sell. Plan: one niche, 5–10 designs, 2–3 products, then
add personalized gift products. Ideas below are starting points, not decisions.

## Niche ideas (buyers in Romania)
- **Romanian humor / slang** — "Las' că merge și așa", sarmale, mici, "Mama zice...".
- **City pride** — minimalist designs for Cluj, Iași, Timișoara, București, Brașov.
- **Modern folk motifs** — ie patterns in a modern style.
- **Hobby niches** — fishing, Carpathian hiking, gaming, a specific dog/cat breed.
- **Family and gifts** — "Cea mai tare mamă/bunică", couple and family sets, birth years.

## Ready-made vs. personalized
| | Ready-made designs | Personalized by the customer |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Low | Medium (configure each product) |
| Price | Normal | Higher (gift premium) |
| Best for | Humor, city, hobby | Gifts: names, dates, family |
| Risk | Easy to copy | Customer typos; EU law excludes personalized goods from the 14-day return right |

## Start plan
1. Launch 5–10 ready-made designs in one niche on t-shirt + hoodie (+ maybe tote bag).
2. Add 2–3 personalized gift products. Gelato+ is already active, so the
   Personalization Studio can be used now ([[gelato-personalization-studio]]).
3. First season to target: **Christmas 2026** — check that ~6-day delivery
   ([[gelato-costs-romania-2026-11]]) still lands before 24 December.

## Price rule of thumb
Price = Gelato product + shipping + margin. Example with list prices:
t-shirt ~62 RON delivered → sell at ~99–129 RON.
