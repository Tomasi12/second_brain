# Collection 01 — Dicționarul Stărilor (feelings)

## Summary
First MATyHOUSE collection: clothes built around feelings. Every design looks like a
**dictionary entry** for one feeling — word, grammar tag, a short warm definition in my own
words, a small line icon. One look for the whole collection, easy to extend.
EN name: *The Dictionary of Feelings*.

## Design system
- Layout: word in large serif (lowercase) → `(s.f.)` / `(s.n.)` tag → 1–2 line definition
  in a light sans → small line icon. Chest print, ~25 cm wide.
- Two colors per print max: black on light shirts, off-white on dark shirts.
- Every definition is **original text** — never copy definitions from DEX or other dictionaries.
- Tone: warm, honest, a bit funny. Never mock sad feelings; no medical claims.
- Each design in RO; best sellers later also in EN.

**Decision 2026-11-19: English only for now** (no RO prints yet). Print files for all 10
are made: `C:\AI_Claude\matyhouse\designs\collection-01\` (transparent PNG, 300 dpi, black
and cream ink each; same type size across designs, so widths vary 11–26 cm — upload at
100%). Generated by `matyhouse\tools\render-dictionary.ps1`; fonts Libre Baskerville + Lato
(OFL, licences in `matyhouse\fonts\`). Mockups: `...\collection-01\mockups\`.

**Print-file rules (learned 2026-11-19):**
- Gelato auto-fits the design to the full print width (~30 cm) → files must stay ≥300 dpi
  at that size. v1 showed "228 dpi"; current files are rendered at 900 dpi design size.
- Gelato prints these hoodies/tees with a **DTF transfer** (white underbase). A first order
  of another design (distressed "SINGUR" text) came out full of white specks and was cancelled.
  Rule: **no semi-transparent pixels** — every pixel 100% ink or 100% transparent, no
  distressed/grunge textures, lines ≥ ~0.7 mm. Current pack: `dictionary-of-feelings_print-files_v3-dtf.zip`,
  checked: 0 semi-transparent pixels, 1 color per file.

How to sell both languages:
- Separate Shopify products per language (Gelato makes one product per design), tagged
  `lang-ro` / `lang-en`; RO and EN of the same design link to each other in the description.
- Sell **both in Romania from day one** — English slogans also sell to young Romanian buyers;
  the sales data shows which language wins.
- Store texts (titles, descriptions) in RO + EN via Translate & Adapt.
