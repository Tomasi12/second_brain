# Gelato — subscription plans

## Summary
Three plans: Free, Gelato+ and Platinum. **The Personalization Studio is only in Gelato+
and Platinum.** I have **Gelato+ Monthly**: €27.99/month in my EUR account, next payment
2026-12-01 (dashboard, 2026-11-19).

## Plans (as published 2026-11-19)
| | Free | Gelato+ | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly billing | $0 | $29.99/month | custom |
| Annual billing | $0 | $19.99/month (saves $120/yr) | custom |
| Product discounts | none | up to 33% (varies by market) | custom |
| Connected stores / users | 2 / 2 | unlimited | unlimited |
| Personalization Studio | no | **yes** | yes |
| Other AI tools (Magic Mockups, Velocity Switch, Price Navigator, Instant Collections) | no | yes | yes |
| Premium stock photos, 800+ premium mockups | no | yes | yes |
| Branded labels / inserts (posters) | no | yes | yes |
| Rewards, implementation service, community events | no | no | yes |

All plans: local production in 32 countries, Shopify/Etsy/API/Order Desk, free mockups, 24/7 support.

## Notes
- Monthly and annual Gelato+ have the same features; only price and billing differ.
- Cancel anytime from the Gelato dashboard.
- Black Friday 2026 offer: code **50PLUS** = 50% off Gelato+ **annual**.
  Worth checking before renewing the monthly plan.
- Large sellers can ask sales for custom pricing, price matching and free samples.
