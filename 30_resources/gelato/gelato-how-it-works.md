# Gelato — how print on demand works

## Summary
Gelato prints and ships custom products on demand near the customer. The seller pays
Gelato only for orders placed: no upfront fees, no commission, no minimum order.

## Order flow
1. Customer orders in my store and pays my price.
2. The order goes to Gelato (automatic via the Shopify app); I pay Gelato product + shipping.
3. Gelato routes it to the production partner nearest the customer, which prints and ships.

## Network
- 250+ production partners in 32 countries, 60+ logistics partners.
- The product catalog shows which product is made locally in which country.
- Live order tracking for the customer; economy and express shipping options.

## Money
- Pay by card or PayPal; multi-currency wallet with optional auto top-up.
- VAT/tax shown at Gelato checkout where it applies.
- Subscriptions unlock product discounts ([[gelato-plans]]).
- Product lines: apparel, calendars, drinkware, wall art, cards, pet items, and more.
  Premium apparel brands listed: AS Colour, Shaka Wear, Stanley/Stella.
