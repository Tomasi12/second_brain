## Using it
- Selling price ≈ total cost + margin; e.g. t-shirt 99–129 RON, hoodie ~199–249 RON.
- Christmas: with ~6 days, the last safe order date is mid-December — check Gelato's
  own holiday cut-off dates before promising delivery.
- Re-check prices before setting final shop prices; they change.
