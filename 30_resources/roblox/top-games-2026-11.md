## Top earners (estimates, not official)
- Trackers based on Roblox's "Top Earning" chart (statroblox, bloxrant) put **RIVALS, Steal An Egg,
  Blox Fruits, Anime Adventures** at the top in Nov 2026. Steal An Egg is estimated at ~$3–4M per 30 days.
- Long-term big earners: Blox Fruits, Brookhaven, Adopt Me!, Pet Simulator 99 (rough estimates of several $M/month each).
- Top earners are **not** always the most played: RIVALS and Anime Adventures earn far more per player than Brookhaven.

## What is "precious" (the patterns)
1. **A 10-second core loop.** Click/throw/collect → reward → upgrade. You understand it with no tutorial.
2. **Collecting + rarity.** Pets, fruits, fish, units, eggs with rare tiers (e.g. 1-in-1000). Rarity creates desire and trading.
3. **Social pressure and interaction.** Stealing from others, trading, roleplaying with friends, PvP. Players bring friends → free growth.
4. **Visible status.** Rare pets, skins and titles that others can see. Most Robux is spent on how you look and what you own.
5. **Fast updates and limited-time events.** Weekly updates, new eggs, seasonal items. FOMO brings players back and drives spending.
6. **Riding a trend fast.** "Steal a ___", "brainrot", fishing, "99 nights". Copies of a winning format (done better/differently) reach the top in weeks.
7. **Short sessions, long progression.** A round lasts minutes, but progression lasts months.
8. **Mobile-first, kid-friendly.** Most players are young and on phones: big buttons, simple UI, clear colors.

## How they make money
- **Game passes** (one-time): 2x money, VIP, extra storage, auto-collect.
- **Developer products** (repeatable): currency packs, eggs/crates, revives, skips. Usually the biggest earner.
- **Gacha / crates / eggs** with rare drops (Pet Sim, Anime Adventures, Steal An Egg).
- **Cosmetics and skins** (RIVALS, MM2) and **battle/season passes**.
- **Premium payouts**: Roblox pays creators for time Roblox Premium members spend in the game.
- **Paid UGC / avatar items** sold in-game (Catalog Avatar Creator).
- Cash-out: Robux → real money through **DevEx** (needs a minimum balance, age and account requirements; check current rules).

## Open questions
- Which genre to start with: a small simulator/tycoon (cheap to build, proven) or a trend copy (fast but crowded)?
- Rules for gacha/loot boxes (Roblox policy requires showing odds; some countries restrict paid random items).
- Ad budget: Roblox sponsored ads / ad credits for the launch.
